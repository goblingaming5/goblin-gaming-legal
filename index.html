Goblin Gaming Studio

Goblin Gaming Studio is a locally operated tool for preparing short gaming videos. It helps a user combine selected gameplay footage with narration and captions, preview the result, and choose whether to share it through supported platforms.

Social-media integrations are subject to each platform's authorization, availability, and approval requirements.
