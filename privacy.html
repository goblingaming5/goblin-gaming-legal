Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how Goblin Gaming Studio ("the App") handles information in its locally operated gaming-video creation workflow. Features described as optional or planned apply only if and when they are enabled.

1. Information handled

The App may process gameplay video files selected by the user, video titles and descriptions, narration scripts, generated audio, captions, render outputs, and local draft and upload-status records. When a supported social-media account is connected, the App may also handle authorization credentials and information needed to submit content to that platform.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and preview videos, save local drafts, authenticate connected accounts, submit user-approved content to supported services, and record upload results. The App does not require users to publish a video merely because it has been rendered.

3. Storage

The current App is designed to run on the user's own computer. Drafts, media, and authorization files may be stored locally in the application's project directories. Users are responsible for securing their computer and keeping credential files private. If hosting, cloud storage, analytics, or additional services are introduced, this policy should be updated before those features are used.

4. Sharing with platforms

When the user expressly initiates a supported upload, the App may send the selected video and relevant metadata to that platform, such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, depending on which integrations are enabled. Those services process information under their own privacy policies. Not all listed integrations are necessarily available or approved.

5. Account authorization

Connected platforms may provide access or refresh tokens to authorize requested actions. Tokens should be stored securely and not placed in public code repositories. Users can revoke a platform's access through that platform's account settings. Revoking access may not automatically erase local draft files or content already published on the platform.

6. Retention and deletion

Local drafts, rendered videos, and saved credentials may remain on the user's computer until the user removes them. To request help with data held by the App operator, contact the address below. Content uploaded to third-party platforms must be managed under those platforms' deletion processes.

7. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect local files and authorization tokens. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, or for users below the applicable minimum age required by a connected platform.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: goblin.gamingttt@gmail.com
